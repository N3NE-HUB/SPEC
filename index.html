What Is a Node Split (Highsplit)?

Cable internet providers like Spectrum (which acquired Time Warner Cable, Charter, and Cox) serve neighborhoods through shared network nodes. As more customers stream and work from home, those nodes become congested.

A node split — also called a Highsplit upgrade — divides a congested node into smaller segments, dramatically improving speeds and reliability for everyone on it. This site helps you find out if that work is underway or coming soon to your area.
